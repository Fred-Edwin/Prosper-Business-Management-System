1. Welcome

Prosper BMS keeps track of your stock and your money across your Restaurant, Canteen, and Store — automatically, every day. You will always know what stock you have, what cash and M-Pesa you should have, and what your business made, without doing the maths yourself.

This guide gets you set up and shows you what to expect on a normal day. It takes about fifteen minutes to read.

3. Three things to understand before you start

These three ideas explain almost everything you will see in Prosper BMS. Read them once and the rest of the app will make sense.

Locations

Your Restaurant, Canteen, and Store each keep their own stock and their own prices. Moving stock from one to another is always recorded as a transfer, so nothing goes missing between them.

Mistakes are easy to fix

If a figure was entered wrong, you can correct it. Open the day it happened, tap the number, and enter the right one. Your totals update straight away.

Handover & closing the day

At the end of each shift, your staff tell Prosper BMS how much cash and M-Pesa they collected. You confirm what you actually received. Once everything for a day is confirmed, you close that day.

4. Setting up for the first time

Do these steps once, in order, before your first real day of business.

  1. Confirm your locations Check that your Restaurant, Canteen, and Store are all set up under Catalog.
  2. Add your products and their prices Add everything you buy, sell, or make, with what it costs and what you sell it for.
  3. Add your staff Go to Staff and add each team member with their role and a starting PIN.
  4. Tell Prosper BMS where you're starting from
    1. a. Count what's currently in stock. Open Ledger from the menu, then Opening Stock, and enter the physical count for every item at every location.
      Opening Stock — enter what you physically have, once.
    2. b. Enter your starting cash and M-Pesa balances. Go to Financials, then Opening Balances.
      Before you set it — an empty form.
      After you save — your Day 1 figures, locked in.
    You do this once. After today, Prosper BMS tracks your money and stock for you. You won't come back to this screen to log today's balance.
  5. You're ready Start recording sales, deliveries, and handovers as they happen.

5. Your daily dashboard

Your dashboard is the first thing you see when you sign in. It's your morning check — a two-minute look before you start the day.

Your dashboard — today's numbers and what needs your attention.
  • Revenue, expenses, and profit — for today, this week, or this month, whichever you choose.
  • Cash at hand and M-Pesa / Bank — your real balances right now.
  • Needs attention — anything waiting on you, like a handover to receive or a day still open.
  • Close today — the switch you use at the end of the day, once everything is confirmed.

7. Closing a day

At the end of each shift, your staff declare the cash and M-Pesa they collected. You then confirm what you actually received before closing the day.

A handover awaiting your receipt — declared amounts on the left, waiting for you to confirm.

If what you received doesn't match what was declared, Prosper BMS shows you the difference so you can follow up before the day is closed. Once every handover for a day is confirmed, close that day from your dashboard.

9. Fixing a mistake

If a figure was entered wrong — a delivery counted short, an issue logged twice — you can fix it yourself from the table.

Correcting a figure in the table

  1. Open Ledger from the menu and choose the day the mistake happened.
  2. Find the row for that item, and tap the number you need to change — in Received, Issued, Non-Sale, or any other column.
  3. Type the correct figure and tap Confirm & Save Correction.
Tap a figure in the table, enter the correct one, and save.

Enter what the figure should be, not the difference. Your stock levels and totals update straight away.

If a single number covers more than one movement that day, tapping it opens the list behind it, so you can pick the exact one to fix.

Correcting your starting balance

If the cash or M-Pesa figure you entered on Day 1 was wrong, open Financials, then Opening Balances, and tap Correct Cash at hand or Correct M-Pesa / Bank.

Correcting a Day 1 figure — a warning appears before you confirm.
This is safe. Every sale, expense, and handover recorded since is completely untouched — correcting it is the same as if you had entered the right number on Day 1.
Only use this to fix a wrong Day 1 figure. It is never how you record today's cash — that happens automatically from your sales, handovers, and expenses.
